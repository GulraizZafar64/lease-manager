SAMPLE CONTRACT (fictional, for trying the import)

LEASE AGREEMENT No. SLA/2026/014

This Lease Agreement is made at Karachi on 15 June 2026 between
Sample Logistics Parks (Private) Limited, hereinafter called the "Lessor"
AND
Demo Distribution (Private) Limited, hereinafter called the "Lessee".

1. PREMISES
The Lessor leases to the Lessee Warehouse No. 4, Plot E-12, Port Qasim Industrial Area, Karachi, having a covered area of 40,000 sq ft (the "Premises"), for use as a storage and distribution warehouse.

2. TERM
The lease shall be for a period of 60 months commencing on 1 July 2026 and expiring on 30 June 2031.

3. RENT
The Lessee shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 2,400,000 (Rupees two million four hundred thousand only), payable monthly in advance on or before the 5th day of each month.

4. ESCALATION
The rent shall increase by 10% every 12 months on each anniversary of the commencement date.

5. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Lessee shall pay a refundable security deposit equal to three months' rent, i.e. Rs. 7,200,000, refundable without interest at the end of the lease.

6. RENEWAL
The Lessee shall have the option to renew this lease for a further period of 36 months on terms to be mutually agreed, by giving 90 days' written notice before expiry.

7. MAINTENANCE AND UTILITIES
Utilities, property tax and routine maintenance are payable by the Lessee in addition to the rent.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first written above.
